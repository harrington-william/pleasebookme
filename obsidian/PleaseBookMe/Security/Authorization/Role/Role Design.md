# Definition

<aside>

💡

  

Roles in PleaseBookMe architecture are separated into 2 levels, which are:

  

- SYSTEM

- BUSINESS

</aside>

---
# System Level

System-level roles are used to control access (RBAC) at platform level, including:

- PLATFORM_OWNER

- PLATFORM_MANAGER

- USER
---
# Business Level

Business-level roles are primarily used to define standardized Role-based Access Control (RBAC) for registered organizations (Tenants). They represents a real business hierarchy, including:

- BUSINESS_OWNER

- BUSINESS_MANAGER

- STAFF

- MEMBER
